FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find your answer? Message us and an attorney will respond.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the contact form below, call our office, or tap the WhatsApp button at the bottom of the screen. We'll confirm a time that suits you.

Is my first conversation confidential?

Yes. Everything you share with us is treated as strictly confidential, even if you decide not to engage the firm.

How are your fees structured?

Depending on the matter, we offer fixed fees, hourly rates or staged billing. You will receive a clear written estimate before any work begins.

Do you assist foreign clients and investors?

Yes. We advise expatriates and international investors on company setup, property, employment and dispute matters.

What documents should I bring?

Bring your ID and any contracts, letters, court papers or correspondence related to your matter. We'll tell you if anything else is needed.
