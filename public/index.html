FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll respond.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the form below, call the office or message us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm a time that suits you.

How does online attestation work?

Upload your document on the attestation page. The attorney reviews it and adds the official stamp. You then receive an email with the fee and bank details. Once you upload your payment slip, you can download the attested PDF.

Can someone check that my attested copy is genuine?

Yes. Every attested copy carries a unique reference number (for example UZHL-XXXXXXXXXX). Anyone can enter it on our verification page to confirm the attestation.

Is my first conversation confidential?

Yes. Everything you share with us is confidential, even if you decide not to engage the firm.

How are your fees structured?

Depending on the matter, we offer fixed fees, hourly rates or staged billing. You receive a written estimate before work begins.

Do you assist foreign clients and investors?

Yes. We advise expatriates and international investors on company setup, property, employment and disputes.
